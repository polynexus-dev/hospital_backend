41 | LOINC Logical Observation Identifiers Names and Codes
Logical Observation Identifiers Names and Codes, or LOINC,
is a universal code system for identifying medical
laboratory observations and clinical measurements. It
is used to standardize the identification of test results and
measurements, which helps to improve the accuracy and
efficiency of medical data exchange between
healthcare organizations and patients. LOINC codes are
used to uniquely identify laboratory and clinical
observations in electronic health records (EHRs), billing
systems, public health reporting, and research studies.

Medical reconciliation Medical reconciliation, often referred to as “med rec”, is a
crucial process in patient care that occurs during transitions
in care—upon hospital admission, transfer from one unit to
another during hospitalization, or discharge from the
hospital to home or another facility.

The process involves reviewing the patient’s complete
medication regimen at the time of admission, transfer, &
discharge and comparing it with the regimen being
considered for the new setting of care. This is done to avoid
inadvertent inconsistencies, such as unintentionally
omitting needed medications, unnecessarily duplicating
existing therapies, or prescribing incorrect dosages.
