The NABH Standards for HIS and EMR systems are designed to complete the loop of digital health standardization
in India. With the 6th edition of hospital accreditation standards focusing on hospital processes, and the Digital Health
Standards driving the digitalization of these processes, the HIS and EMR standards now aim to standardize the
products and systems used in hospitals. Together, these efforts are building a robust and well-integrated digital
healthcare ecosystem across the country.
