Core m. The system links the radiology report/s of the patient to their ABHA.
The system shall link a patient’s radiology reports to their ABHA. Linking a
patient’s radiology reports to their ABHA number/Address makes the information
more shareable and helps healthcare providers to have a complete and accurate
