Core e. The system is capable of taking a backup/ archiving old data.
The system shall be capable of data backup/ archive, empowering administrators to
systematically retain and access data for a specified retention period depending on
the law of the state or healthcare organization requirements (e.g., 5 years or as
’ notified by state laws). This will help the system to adhere to the data compliance
CCU requirements as per industry best practices.
The system shall be capable of retrieving and restoring the backup whenever
needed. The system shall follow the data backup/archiving policy/SOP as
documented by the healthcare organization.
