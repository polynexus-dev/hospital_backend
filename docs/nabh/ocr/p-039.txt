Core f. The system links the discharge summary of the patient with their ABHA.
The system shall link a patient’s discharge summary to their ABHA. Going
forward, healthcare providers treating a patient can access their discharge

Interpretation summary to get a comprehensive overview of the patient's medical history,
including any critical/ sensitive health issues and medications from the past.
This corresponds to Milestone 2 (M2) of ABDM.
