Core h. | The system clearly marks the damaged/ rejected samples.
The system shall have the capability to mark a sample as damaged or rejected along
with the reason. This could include adding a specific code or annotation to indicate
the sample’s status. For example, appending “DAMAGED” or “REJECTED” to the
sample ID can help clearly distinguish it from other samples. This ensures that the
samples are not used for further testing.
Interpretation
Indicators: The system could use color coding/ icons to visually highlight damaged
or spoiled samples. For example:
e Red labels or tags could indicate damaged samples.
e Yellow labels or tags could indicate samples that need retesting due to spoilage.
e Green labels or tags could represent valid samples.
