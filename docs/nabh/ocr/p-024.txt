The healthcare organization requires a system to manage queues for various
healthcare services such as patient registration, OPD, pharmacy, laboratory,
radiology, etc.
The system may be able to manage and monitor patient types like - scheduled
visits, walk-in, new visits, revisits, etc.

Interpretation details with the healthcare facility. Patients receive a token number against their
transaction which is shared with the data entry operator of a Health facility. This
makes patient registration faster and also reduces the chances of demographic
error.
