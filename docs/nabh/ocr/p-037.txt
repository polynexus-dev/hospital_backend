NABH
Excellence i. The system has the capability to predict bed availability.
From the data available in the system, the system shall be able to predict how the
bed availability will change over the next few days / weeks.
Interpretation
This information can be very useful during extreme situations ( eg., during dengue
outbreaks, pandemics) and for business / capacity planning purposes.
Standard
mace The system manages patient discharge and transfer processes.
Objective Elements
Core a. The system creates / modifies a discharge summary.
The system shall have predefined templates for the discharge summary. The
template for discharge summary should include (but not limited to):
e Patient's name
e Unique identification number
e Name of the treating doctor
e Date of admission and date of discharge
: e Reasons for admission
Interpretation
¢ Significant findings, diagnosis and patient's condition at time of discharge
e Information regarding investigation results
e Any procedure performed
e Medication administered
e Any other treatment given
Coreyiliticalaeme| The system shows the list of patients due for discharge.
The system shall show the list of patients due for discharge on a daily basis. The
discharges like LAMA, DAMA, and absconding shall be clearly marked in the
Interpretation system and a notification regarding the same should be sent to the relevant
departments and staff.
