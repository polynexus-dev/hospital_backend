The system shall assist healthcare providers in recognizing patients who are
vulnerable to adverse health outcomes. Vulnerable patients might include those
with chronic illnesses, the elderly, individuals with multiple comorbidities, or those
with socioeconomic factors that affect their health.
The system shall also be able to assist healthcare providers in recognizing

Branden Scale, The European and US National pressure ulcer Advisory panels
(EPUAP and NPUAP) staging systems.
