Commitment a The system electronically computes and publishes Key Performance Indicators
(KPIs) per NABH accreditation standards for hospitals and healthcare organizations.
The system captures relevant patient and administrative data, and computes KPIs
as per NABH accreditation standards for hospitals.
The system must have the ability to compute the KPls based on end-user defined
periods (start/end dates) and export the KPIs and underlying computation to end
. users for further analysis (in JSON, .csv, .xml, .xls, .pdf formats).
Interpretation

standards is given in Annexure-8.
