The system shall provide the functionality for medical professionals to view
captured images from multiple modalities, radiologist reports, readings and
annotations relevant to the encounter and historical images.
Medical imaging plays an instrumental role in diagnostics and quality of care.
With increasing use of medical imaging, access to medical images along with
clinical data of patients helps physicians provide better care.
