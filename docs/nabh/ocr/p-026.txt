Core c. The system tracks specimens.
With a unique identifier assigned to each sample, lab technicians can easily track
and manage the sample, improving efficiencies, and reducing the risk of mix-ups
or errors. The key features of specimen tracking include splitting orders, progress
Interpretation tracking, and data review & approval.
Tracking allows increased visibility of the status of the sample, for example
whether the sample has been processed rejected, or there is a mismatch, etc.
