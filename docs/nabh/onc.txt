                                          Introduction

Cancer is one of the leading causes of morbidity and mortality worldwide, and India is witnessing a
rapidly growing cancer burden. With increasing incidence, late-stage presentations, and the complexity
of oncology care, the disease poses profound challenges for patients, clinicians, and the healthcare
system as a whole. Oncology, by its very nature, requires multidisciplinary collaboration, longitudinal
care, and integration of advanced diagnostics, therapeutics, and follow-up pathways.

In this context, digital health technologies--particularly HIS (Hospital Information Systems) and EMRs
(Electronic Medical Records)--play a pivotal role in streamlining oncology workows, enhancing
clinical decision-making, ensuring continuity of care, and supporting evidence generation.
Standardized oncology-specic functionalities within HIS/EMRs are therefore essential to enable
clinicians, care teams, and health systems to deliver comprehensive, high-quality, and patient-centered
cancer care.

This annexure on Oncology HIS/EMR serves as a specialty certication in addition to the NABH Digital
Health Standards for HIS/EMR Systems. It denes oncology-specic capabilities and workows
required for effective and essential cancer care delivery in India, covering aspects such as
multidisciplinary tumor boards, treatment planning, chemotherapy and radiotherapy management,
survivorship care, and long-term follow-up. By complementing the HIS/EMR certication, this annexure
ensures that oncology care providers are equipped with digital systems aligned to the unique needs of
oncology practice.

While these standards have been developed through extensive internal deliberations and consultation
with oncology experts at National Cancer Grid (NCG) and digital health stakeholders, NABH recognizes
that oncology is a rapidly evolving domain. Continuous engagement and feedback from Oncologists,
cancer care institutions, digital health companies, and other stakeholders will be critical to rening and
updating these standards in alignment with emerging best practices and innovations.

Through this initiative, NABH reafrms its commitment to advancing excellence in oncology care,
strengthening digital health infrastructure, and fostering a more integrated, efcient, and patient-centric
cancer care ecosystem in India.

Intent of the annexure

An HIS/EMR system designed for oncology care must capture comprehensive patient information,
including demographic details, medical and surgical history, medication records, and laboratory results.
Given the chronic and complex nature of cancer, the system should also facilitate the capture of
longitudinal data to enable an integrated and coordinated approach to treatment. This oncology-specic
annexure builds upon the existing 1st edition NABH Digital Health Standards for HIS/EMR Systems and
Objective Elements (OEs) by incorporating oncology-focused enhancements, as well as introducing
new standards and OEs tailored to the unique requirements of cancer care and management. The
HIS/EMR companies applying for Oncology certication should comply with the enhancements to the
existing standards and new standards as per the compliance level mandated by NABH.
